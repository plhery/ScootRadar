# Japan scooter feeds

Verified 24 October 2026 through Lime's authenticated rider map. The catalog
registers the complete published operating polygons for **Naha**,
partitioned into small independent cache feeds. These groups count as one
destination; they are not separate towns.

The region is `N67TS4UDQBFZG`. Its public outline document is:

- [Publisher service polygons](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Naha_188_exclude_new_type.json)

Lime's scooters in Tokyo and Yokohama, about 2,280 and 50, were added on
26 October 2026 the same way, each market whole after a clean full-area audit
that morning; see
[the Lime guide](lime-app-map.md#every-market-the-map-lists-october-2026).
Both are zones of Lime's Tokyo region, `MDH3CPXCIE5F3`. Its other zones, in
Chiba and in Kawasaki's Takatsu ward, held no scooter in the audit and are
left out, as is a test zone. Its public outline document is:

- [Tokyo region service polygons](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Tokyo_1355_exclude_new_type.json)

The market uses `Asia/Tokyo`. Only scooter pins inside the recorded
service polygons are included. Bikes, fares and vehicle-specific rental links
are excluded. Public feeds remain preferred wherever available.
