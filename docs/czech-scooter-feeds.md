# Czechia scooter feeds

Verified 24 October 2026 through Lime's authenticated rider map. The catalog
registers the complete published operating polygons for **České Budějovice**,
partitioned into small independent cache feeds. These groups count as one
destination; they are not separate towns.

Update, 25 October 2026: **Brno** and **Ostrava** are registered the same
way, each after a clean full-area audit, with about 510 and 300 scooters.
Lime's Praha, Liberec and Olomouc markets held no scooter in their whole
scooter zones that day: Prague has banned shared scooters since 1 January
2026.

The regions and their public outline documents:

- České Budějovice, `N7I62XA6DMPA7`: [publisher service polygons](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Ceske_Budejovice_108_exclude_new_type.json)
- Brno, `4GBGQCKC4FGYQ`: [publisher service polygons](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Brno_95_exclude_new_type.json)
- Ostrava, `LIF6QZBU2XHV3`: [publisher service polygons](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Ostrava_111_exclude_new_type.json)

The markets use `Europe/Prague`. Only scooter pins inside the recorded
service polygons are included. Bikes, fares and vehicle-specific rental links
are excluded. Public feeds remain preferred wherever available.

See [Lime collection and session requirements](lime-app-map.md) for the local
view protocol, completeness checks, request quota and refresh cadence.
