# Mexico scooter feeds

Verified 24 October 2026 through Lime's authenticated rider map. The catalog
registers the complete published operating polygons for **Querétaro**,
partitioned into small independent cache feeds. These groups count as one
destination; they are not separate towns.

The region is `ZNW45XILOWWZ4`. Its public outline document is:

- [Publisher service polygons](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Queretaro_158_exclude_new_type.json)

Lime's scooters in Monterrey and Guadalupe, about 2,910, were added on
26 October 2026 the same way, after a clean full-area audit that evening,
Mexico City time; see
[the Lime guide](lime-app-map.md#every-market-the-map-lists-october-2026).
The region is `OCZC3MAHZCAEW`. Its two zones, named Guadalupe and Monterrey,
draw the same outline across both municipalities, so the map lists them as
Monterrey, and searching Guadalupe finds it. Its public outline document is:

- [Monterrey region service polygons](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Monterrey_340_exclude_new_type.json)

Lime's Ciudad de México and León regions held no scooter in their audits
that evening: SEMOVI's Mexico City scooter pilot ended on 31 August 2026 (see
[coverage gaps](coverage-gaps.md)).

The market uses `America/Mexico_City`. Only scooter pins inside the recorded
service polygons are included. Bikes, fares and vehicle-specific rental links
are excluded. Public feeds remain preferred wherever available.

view protocol, completeness checks, request quota and refresh cadence.
