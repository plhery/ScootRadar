ScootRadar puts every shared e-scooter around you on one map, whoever runs it, so the closest ride is the first thing you see.

Each operator's app shows only its own fleet. ScootRadar shows them all, so the nearest scooter is simply the nearest one.

KNOW THE WALK
Distance and walking time from where you stand, or from an address you look up. Directions draws the way on foot on the map and follows you as you walk.

SKIP THE FLAT ONES
Battery and remaining range, where the operator publishes them. Set a minimum and the map keeps only the scooters that reach it.

PRICE BEFORE YOU COMMIT
An estimate from the operator's published rates for a ride of 5 to 30 minutes, with the free unlocks and free minutes of your passes taken into account. The operator's app shows the final price.

ONE TAP TO THE RIGHT APP
"Open in" takes you to the operator's own app, where you unlock and pay as usual. ScootRadar doesn't hire out or reserve scooters.

WHERE IT WORKS
The scooters of 35 operators, in more than 800 towns and cities in 46 countries: Switzerland, France, Germany, Italy, the United Kingdom, Belgium, Spain, Poland, Norway, Sweden, Finland and more of Europe; the United States and Canada; Brazil, Chile and Mexico; Japan, South Korea and Australia; Israel, Turkey, New Zealand and others. Among them are Berlin, Oslo, Tokyo, Chicago, Santiago, Rome, São Paulo, Stockholm, Seoul and Zürich. Where no operator publishes its data, the map says so and shows the closest cities that do.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no ads. Your location is used on your iPhone to centre the map and work out walking times; the server only receives the part of the map you are looking at. Anonymous usage statistics can be turned off in Settings.

ALSO
• Light and dark appearance, and Standard, Quiet and Satellite maps
• Parking bays where an operator requires them
• Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion
• English, German, French, Italian, Dutch, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish and Icelandic

Positions come from the operators and public authorities, and can be a few minutes old.
