# Romania scooter feeds

Verified 24 October 2026 through Lime's authenticated rider map. The catalog
registers the complete published operating polygons for **Deva**,
partitioned into small independent cache feeds. These groups count as one
destination; they are not separate towns.

Update, 25 October 2026: **București**, **Cluj-Napoca**, **Iași**, **Alba
Iulia**, **Sibiu**, **Târgu Mureș** and **Târgoviște** are registered the same
way, each after a clean full-area audit, with about 7,000 scooters, 4,950 of
them in București. Lime's Arad, Brașov, Constanța and Galați markets held no
scooter in their whole scooter zones that day, and Oradea's a single one;
check Constanța, on the Black Sea, again in summer.
