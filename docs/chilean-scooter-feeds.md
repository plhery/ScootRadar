# Chile scooter feeds

Verified 24 October 2026 through Lime's authenticated rider map. The catalog
registers the complete published operating polygons for **Santiago**,
partitioned into small independent cache feeds. These groups count as one
destination; they are not separate towns.

The region is `WT2NYSACDT47J`. Its public outline document is:

- [Publisher service polygons](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Santiago_379_exclude_new_type.json)

The market uses `America/Santiago`. Only scooter pins inside the recorded
service polygons are included. Bikes, fares and vehicle-specific rental links
are excluded. Public feeds remain preferred wherever available.

See [Lime collection and session requirements](lime-app-map.md) for the local
view protocol, completeness checks, request quota and refresh cadence.

Lime's reference point is inside the main northeastern operating area. Its
polygons also cover the separately recorded southern districts; downtown
Santiago is outside those polygons. The shared city shortcut keeps its
existing centre for the other operators.

## Viña del Mar, October 2026

Lime's map lists a second Chilean region, Viña del Mar's, which its
locations page doesn't name: it was found on 27 October 2026 by reading the
map's static elements at Viña del Mar's centre. Its one scooter zone,
"Vina del Mar SZ V1.1", covers central Viña del Mar, about 6 km by 4 km,
and held 295 scooters in its full-area audit that afternoon, every stationary
reference found. It is registered as its own market, beside Whoosh's
scooters there, from its
[outline document](https://assets.lime.bike/zones_attributes/Vina_del_Mar_32_exclude_new_type.json).
No night ban or season was found for Viña del Mar.

## Night bans, October 2026

No night ban was found anywhere the map shows Chilean scooters (checked
28 October 2026), so the catalog sets none:

- **National law:** [Ley 21.088](https://www.leychile.cl/Consulta/obtxml?opt=7&idLey=21088)
  (2018), which regulates scooters, sets no hours for renting or riding them.
  Its regulations ask for reflective gear between dusk and dawn, which is not
  a ban.
- **Whoosh** (Santiago, La Serena, Puerto Montt, Rancagua, Pucón, Viña del Mar
  and Limache): its [terms for Chile](https://whoosh.bike/terms_cl/es),
  amended 29 December 2025, set no hours, only a four-hour longest rental.
  Limache's mayor says its service runs "24/7"
  ([El Proa](https://elproa.cl/2026/06/limache-incorpora-150-scooters-electricos-y-100-bicicletas-para-conectar-barrios-comercio-y-metro-valparaiso/),
  17 June 2026).
- **La Serena, Rancagua and Puerto Montt:** the rules their agreements set
  (parking, age, helmets) include no hours, according to the press and
  [Puerto Montt's own page](https://www.puertomontt.cl/puerto-montt-cuenta-con-plan-piloto-de-electromovilidad-con-250-scooters-electricos-en-arriendo/)
  (13 April 2026).
- **Santiago's comunas and Temuco:** their permits and tenders aren't
  published, so they weren't read. Press coverage of scooters in Las Condes,
  Vitacura, Lo Barnechea, Providencia, Ñuñoa, San Miguel and Temuco in 2024–26
  (thefts, tenders, a bill in Congress) mentions no night ban. Lo Barnechea
  leads a joint tender with Vitacura and Las Condes for about 1,800 scooters,
  to be awarded in November 2026
  ([La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/pulso/noticia/licitacion-de-scooters-de-lo-barnechea-vitacura-y-las-condes-enfrenta-a-dos-gigantes-de-estados-unidos-y-rusia/),
  3 September 2026): check its rules when they are published, and Temuco's.
